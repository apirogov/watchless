- [procedure] Method 1: wrap the mold in plastic, roll a 1/4-in slab, fit/trim a template, bevel with the short 45° side, score/slip, wrap and rib the seam, attach a slab floor, remove the mold, then smooth the inner seam and rim.
- [procedure] Method 2: start with 1 lb of soft wedged clay in a cylinder, pierce it with a small dowel and enlarge with successively wider dowels while rolling, re-wet stretch cracks with a damp sponge, fit the plastic-wrapped mold, and attach/trim the bottom.
- [procedure] For handles, roll a 1/4-in slab, cut a strip with a metal ruler, bevel both edges with a fingernail, drape it over a foam mold, trim, and attach at leather hard.
