# Corpus

Recorded inputs for the prompt-tuning harness in `tests/bench/`. Not shipped in
the sdist or the wheel, and not part of the package.

## What these files are

Each `*.md` is a real `watchless` output, generated by fetching a public YouTube
video and summarising it with an LLM endpoint. They are committed because they
are the evidence behind every change to `src/watchless/prompts/summarize.md`:
without a fixed set of inputs, "the prompt got better" is an opinion. Regenerating
them costs real YouTube requests against a rate-limited IP, so they are expensive
to reproduce and not disposable.

`REPORT.md` and `resolved.json` are generated by `tests/bench/corpus.py`.
The `*.log` files are per-run noise and are gitignored.

## Provenance and copyright

The transcripts in these files are YouTube's speech-to-text captions for videos
by third parties — ABC News, ZDF, Dr. Eric Berg DC and others, identified by URL
and channel in each file's header. **The underlying video content belongs to its
uploaders.** It is reproduced here as test fixtures, in the same way a test suite
reproduces a few lines of a page it parses, and no licence is claimed over it.

If you are a rights holder and want a file removed, open an issue at
<https://github.com/apirogov/watchless/issues>; it will be dropped from the corpus
and the corresponding test case retired.

## Why it is excluded from distributions

Two reasons, and both are the kind that only bite after publication:

- These are third-party captions. Redistributing them inside a wheel puts
  someone else's text into every `pip install`.
- They are tuning material. A package downloader wants to convert a video, not
  to re-run a prompt against 17 recorded ones.
